Our Menu

From classic ice cream truck favorites to refreshing slushies and frozen treats, there’s something for everyone at Lil’s Ice Cream.

Whether you’re craving a childhood favorite, cooling off with one of our signature slushies, or trying something new, we’ve carefully selected treats that make every stop memorable. Our menu is always stocked with the favorites you know and love, making us the perfect stop for birthdays, neighborhood events, schools, businesses, festivals, and community gatherings.

Cold. Refreshing. Fun. Every visit.

Menu at a glance

Ice cream sandwiches, chocolate-coated ice cream bars, strawberry shortcake bars, orange cream bars, ice cream cookie sandwiches, blue raspberry and cherry Two-Ball Screwballs, cones, character pops and frozen cups.

Bomb Pops: Unicorn Wonder, Watermelon, Original and Candy Clash. Frozen treats also include mango, bubble gum and cotton candy pops, plus Snickers, Twix and Butterfinger ice cream bars.

Snowie flavors: Cherry, Lime, Blue Raspberry, Grape, Piña Colada and Cotton Candy. Slushies are also available.

For help with the menu, current prices, ingredients or availability, email customerservice@lilsicecream.com.
