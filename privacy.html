Privacy Policy

Lil’s Ice Cream App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 4-21-2025

Lil’s Ice Cream is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Lil’s Ice Cream mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use the app, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and street address (entered during registration)
  • Device location (to show you the truck’s location and allow pin requests)
  • Push notification token (used to send alerts about the truck’s activity)
  • Pin drop requests (stored with time, location, and user information to help us respond)

We do not collect or store any financial information.


How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Display the truck’s real-time location on the map
  • Send notifications when the truck is nearby
  • Process pin drop requests
  • Support event bookings and customer service

All data is stored securely using Apple’s CloudKit platform and is never sold or shared with third parties.


Your Choices

  • You may update or delete your profile at any time within the app.
  • You can disable location access or push notifications in your device settings.
  • You may delete your account, which will permanently remove your data.

Third-Party Services

This app relies on Apple services including:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) for location display
  • CloudKit for data storage
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) for notifications

We do not use third-party tracking or analytics services.


Children’s Privacy

Lil’s Ice Cream does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it promptly.
