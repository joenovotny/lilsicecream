Lil’s Ice Cream Android App Data Deletion

Request deletion of data associated with the Lil’s Ice Cream Android app. Please include the name, email address, or phone number you used in the app so we can locate your records.

Request data deletion by email

Opens your email app with a request template. Send the email to submit your request. Alternatively, email customerservice@lilsicecream.com with the subject “Android app data deletion request.”

App name: Lil’s Ice Cream

Developer: Novotny Concessions LLC

Data that can be deleted

Upon request, we will delete or anonymize app data associated with your profile, including your name, email address, phone number, address or neighborhood information, location pins or “Wave Us Down” requests, and push notification token.

Data that may be retained

We may retain limited information if required for legal, safety, fraud prevention, accounting, or business record purposes.

Deletion timeline

We will review and process deletion requests within 30 days.
